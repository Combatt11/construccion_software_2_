# Requirements — Nexora

## Purpose

Document the functional needs of the Nexora marketplace.

## Stakeholders

- Buyer
- Seller
- Administrator
- Logistics Operator
- Supervisor

## Main functionalities

- User and role management.
- Buyer management.
- Seller onboarding and management.
- Product and category management.
- Warehouse management.
- Inventory management.
- Cart and order management.
- Invoicing.
- Order preparation and dispatch.
- Returns and refunds.

## Product types

- Physical: requires inventory and dispatch.
- Digital: can be delivered immediately after payment.

> This document is an initial organization of the functional content provided for the project.
